Frequently Asked Questions

Is RenterAI really free?
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no sign-up, no hidden fees. Our core tools will always remain free.
Is my lease information stored anywhere?
No. Your lease text and personal information are never stored on our servers. Everything is processed in real-time and immediately discarded.
Is this legal advice?
No. RenterAI provides general legal information for educational purposes only. We are not a law firm. For serious legal matters, please consult a licensed attorney.
Which states does RenterAI cover?
All 50 US states. Our AI is trained on federal landlord-tenant law as well as state-specific laws across the entire United States.
How accurate is the AI?
Our AI is highly accurate for general legal information, but laws change frequently. Always verify important legal information with your state's official resources or a local attorney.
