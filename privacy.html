🔒 Short version: RenterAI does not require an account and does not sell your personal information for money. Text you enter into our AI tools is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a response, and we use analytics and advertising services described below. 1. Who We Are

RenterAI ("we," "us") operates the website www.renteraiapp.com, which offers free tools, calculators and guides for renters in the United States. You can reach us through our Contact page.

2. Information We Collect

Information you enter into our AI tools. When you use a tool such as the Lease Analyzer, Demand Letter Writer, Tenant Rights Checker, Security Deposit Checker, Rent Increase Analyzer or Roommate Agreement Generator, the text and details you enter are sent through our server to Google's Gemini API, which generates the response. Our code does not save this content to a database. Google processes it under its own terms, and our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as request time and errors).

Calculators. The numbers you enter into our calculators are calculated in your browser. If you click an "AI explanation" button, the results and any names you typed are sent to Gemini in the same way as above.

Contact form. If you write to us, we receive your name, email address and message through our form provider (Web3Forms) so we can reply.

Usage data. We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. These services may collect your IP address, device and browser details, approximate location, pages viewed, and interactions such as clicks and scrolling. Clarity may record how you interact with a page to produce heatmaps and session replays.
