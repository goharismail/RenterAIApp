Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

🔒 Short version: We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Your lease text and information entered into our tools is never saved anywhere. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Information We Collect

RenterAI is designed with privacy as a core principle. Here is what we collect and what we do not:

What we do NOT collect:

  • Lease agreement text you paste into our tools
  • Your name, address, or personal details entered into tools
  • Any output generated by our AI tools
  • Payment information (our tools are free)

What we may collect:

  • Basic anonymous usage analytics (page views, tool usage counts)
  • Browser type and general location (country/state level only)
  • Error logs for debugging purposes (no personal data included)

2. How Our Tools Work

When you use RenterAI tools, your input is sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API for processing. This communication happens in real-time and the content is not stored on our servers.

Please review Google's privacy policy at ai.google.dev to understand how they handle API requests.

Your API key is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for the website to function properly. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymous usage statistics only (no personal data)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Google AdSense

RenterAI displays advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show you relevant ads based on your browsing history. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

RenterAI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using our Contact page.
