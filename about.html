About Us

Helping Renters Fight Back

We believe every renter deserves to understand their rights — regardless of income or legal knowledge.

"Most renters sign a lease without ever having a lawyer look at it. RenterAI exists to make rental information easier to understand."

Our Mission

RenterAI builds free tools, calculators and plain-English guides that help American renters understand their leases, their costs and the rules that may apply to them.

Many renters are unsure what a clause means, how a deposit should be returned, or how to split costs fairly with roommates. We aim to give clear starting points, and to point you to official sources and legal aid when you need more than general information.

What We Offer

🤖
AI Tools
Lease analysis, demand letters, tenant rights, security deposits, rent increases and roommate agreements, with AI-generated drafts and explanations.
🧮
Calculators
Free rent calculators for splitting rent, prorating rent, and planning costs.

Who We Are

RenterAI is run by the RenterAI Editorial Team, a small independent team. We are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice. You can read how we research and check our content in our Editorial Policy, and you can reach us anytime through the Contact page.

Our Principles

Free to use: Our tools do not require payment or sign-up.

Honest about limits: AI and general guides can be wrong or out of date. We say so, and we ask you to verify important details.

We fix mistakes: If you find an error, tell us and we will review it and correct it.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

RenterAI provides general information for educational purposes only. We are not a law firm, and our tools do not constitute legal advice. For advice about your situation, contact a licensed attorney or a legal aid organization.
