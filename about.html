About Us

Helping Renters Fight Back

We believe every renter deserves to understand their rights — regardless of income or legal knowledge.

"44 million American households rent their homes. Most of them have never read their full lease — and landlords know it. RenterAI exists to change that."

Our Mission

RenterAI was built with one simple goal: give everyday American renters access to the same quality of legal information that wealthy tenants get from expensive attorneys.

Most renters don't know their rights. They sign leases with hidden clauses. They lose security deposits they're legally owed. They get illegally evicted because they don't know how to fight back.

Our free AI-powered tools change that — instantly, at no cost, and without any sign-up required.

What We Do

We build free, AI-powered tools specifically designed for USA renters:

Lease Analysis

Our Values

Free Forever: Our core tools will always be free. We believe access to legal information should not be gated by income.

No Sign-Up Required: We don't ask for your email, name, or any personal information. Just use the tools and go.

Privacy First: Your lease text and personal details are never stored, sold, or shared. Period.

Accuracy Matters: We constantly improve our AI prompts and tools to ensure the most accurate, up-to-date legal information possible.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

RenterAI provides general legal information for educational purposes only. We are not a law firm, and our tools do not constitute legal advice. Laws vary by state and locality. For serious legal matters, please consult a licensed attorney or your local legal aid society.
