What Does It Cost to Move Into a Rental?

The monthly rent is only part of what you need before you get the keys. Most renters pay a combination of housing costs due to the landlord, moving costs, and the cost of setting up the new home. This calculator adds them together so you can see how much cash you need up front.

Upfront Housing Costs

  • First month's rent: usually due at or before move-in, sometimes prorated if you move in mid-month. See the Prorated Rent Calculator.
  • Security deposit: money held against damage or unpaid rent. The amount and rules depend on your state and lease.
  • Last month's rent: required by some landlords. Ask whether it is applied to your final month.
  • Pet deposits or fees, application fees, admin or broker fees: these vary by property and city. Ask for a full list of fees in writing.
  • Renters insurance: some leases require it. Learn more in our renters insurance guide.

Moving Costs

Moving costs depend on distance, the amount you are moving, the time of year and whether you hire help or do it yourself. If you hire movers or rent a truck, get written quotes, ask what is included, and ask whether a quote is binding or an estimate that can change. Add packing supplies, fuel, tolls, storage and any cleaning at your old or new home.

Setting Up the New Home

Utilities may require a deposit or connection fee, internet may need installation or equipment, and a new home often needs furniture and basic household items. These costs are easy to forget, so include them in your budget.

Tips for Budgeting a Move

  • Ask the landlord for a written list of everything due at lease signing and at move-in.
  • Collect at least two moving quotes before choosing.
  • Add a cushion. Moves often cost more than the first estimate.
  • Use our move-in checklist to document the condition of the home before you unpack.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to move into an apartment?

It varies widely by city, building and how you move. Add up your actual lease amounts and quotes: first month's rent, deposit, any other fees, moving costs and setup costs. The calculator totals them for you.

What are move-in costs?

Move-in costs are the money due to the landlord before you move in, such as first month's rent, a security deposit and fees. Many people also include moving costs and setup costs in their move-in budget.

Do I have to pay first and last month's rent?

Some landlords ask for first and last month's rent, and some ask for first month's rent plus a deposit. Requirements depend on the landlord, your lease and local law, so check your lease and ask for the amounts in writing.

How much extra should I budget for a move?

Many people add a cushion of 10% or more to cover surprises. The calculator starts with 10%, and you can change it.

Are broker or admin fees common?

They exist in some cities and at some properties, but they are not universal. Ask whether any broker, admin or lease fees apply before you apply.

Is the security deposit part of my moving costs?

It is a cost you pay up front, but it is generally refundable at the end of the lease, minus any lawful deductions. See our security deposit guide for how returns work.
