About Our Content

Editorial Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

📝 Short version: RenterAI provides general information and tools for renters, not legal advice. This page explains how we research our guides, how our AI tools work and where they can be wrong, and how to report a mistake.

Who Writes RenterAI

Our guides and tools are produced by the RenterAI Editorial Team, a small independent team. We are not a law firm, and unless a page says otherwise, our guides have not been reviewed by a licensed attorney.

How We Research Our Guides

  • We start from state statutes and official government, court and legal aid sources whenever we can, and name the relevant statute where we are confident of it.
  • We use AI tools to help draft and organize text, and our team edits it. AI can make mistakes, so we check high-impact details such as deadlines, deposit limits and notice periods against official sources.
  • We write for a general reader. Laws differ by city and county, and our guides cannot cover every local rule.

How Our AI Tools Work

Our AI tools send what you enter to Google's Gemini model, which writes a response. We instruct the model not to invent laws, citations, deadlines or amounts, not to give legal advice and not to tell you that something is definitely legal or illegal. The model can still be wrong or out of date, so every result carries a reminder to verify important details with an official source, a legal aid organization or a licensed attorney.

Our calculators use fixed formulas that run in your browser. Results are estimates based on the numbers you enter and any assumptions shown on the page.

Corrections

If you find an error, please tell us through our Contact page and choose "Report incorrect information." We review reports, fix confirmed mistakes, and mark corrected guides with a "Corrected" note.

Recent corrections (October 2026): we corrected statements in our Colorado, Indiana, Maryland, Nevada and New Jersey tenant rights guides, including security deposit rules, notice periods for ending month-to-month tenancies, and rent increase notice rules.

Updates

Landlord-tenant laws change. We update a guide when we learn of a change or an error, but we cannot promise that every page reflects the very latest law. Please confirm important details before acting.

Advertising and Independence

RenterAI is free to use and may be supported by advertising. Advertisers do not review or control our content, and we do not accept payment to change what our guides say.
