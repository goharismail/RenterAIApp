What Does an AI Lease Analyzer Check?

A lease agreement can contain dozens of terms covering rent, deposits, fees, repairs, renewals, termination, pets, guests, and other rules. RenterAI helps renters review those terms in plain English before signing or while reviewing an existing rental agreement.

Paste your lease into the analyzer and look for clauses that may deserve closer attention. The tool can help highlight unusual fees, restrictive rules, unclear language, renewal terms, termination provisions, and other important sections of your rental agreement.

Common Lease Red Flags to Look For

1. Unclear or Unexpected Fees

Check your lease for application fees, administrative fees, late fees, cleaning charges, maintenance fees, or other costs that you did not expect.

2. Automatic Renewal Clauses

Some leases automatically renew unless the tenant gives notice within a specific period. Check your agreement carefully for renewal deadlines and notice requirements.

3. Early Termination Rules

Review what your lease says about moving out early, notice periods, termination fees, and other conditions that may apply.

4. Maintenance and Repair Responsibilities

Check which repairs the landlord is responsible for and which responsibilities are assigned to the tenant.

5. Security Deposit Terms

Look for information about the deposit amount, deductions, move-out requirements, and the conditions for receiving your deposit back.

Before You Sign a Rental Lease

Don't focus only on the monthly rent. Review the entire agreement, including fees, renewal terms, maintenance responsibilities, guest policies, pets, utilities, termination rules, and security deposit provisions.

If a clause is unclear or you believe a term may conflict with local tenant protections, consider asking the landlord for clarification or getting advice from a qualified housing professional or attorney.
