Cookie Policy

How RenterAI uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

📅 Last Updated: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how RenterAI ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit www.renteraiapp.com. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the website owners about how visitors use the site.

Cookies are not programs and cannot carry viruses or install malware. They cannot access any other information on your device beyond what was placed there by the website you are visiting.

Types of Cookies We Use

Specific Technologies We Use

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, time on site, and approximate geographic location. This data is aggregated and anonymized — it does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Media.net

We may use Media.net to serve contextual advertisements. Media.net uses cookies to display ads relevant to the content you are viewing. For more information, see Media.net's Privacy Policy.

Quge5

We use Quge5 as an additional advertising partner. This service may place cookies on your device to serve relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics, deliver advertisements, and so forth. These third parties include advertising networks, analytics providers, and social media platforms. Third-party cookies are governed by the respective privacy policies of those third parties — we do not control these cookies and they are subject to the third party's own privacy policies.

🔒 Your Privacy Matters: We do not sell your personal data. Cookies used for advertising help keep RenterAI free for all renters. We use the minimum number of cookies necessary to operate our website and fund its operation through advertising.

How to Control Cookies

You have several options for controlling or limiting how cookies are used on your device:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that if you disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly. Here are links to cookie settings for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many advertisers through the following industry opt-out tools:

  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) Opt-Out
  • Your Online Choices (EU)

Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Our website currently does not respond to DNT signals in a standardized way, as there is no industry-wide standard for DNT compliance. We will update this policy if a standard is established.

Cookie Consent

When you first visit RenterAI, you may see a cookie consent banner. By continuing to use our website after seeing this banner, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or using the opt-out tools listed above.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
