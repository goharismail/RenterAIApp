💰 Your Rent Split

What Is a Rent Split Calculator?

A rent split calculator helps roommates figure out exactly how much each person should pay toward the total monthly rent. Instead of guessing or splitting everything evenly by default, it lets you choose a method that reflects how the space and cost are actually shared.

Rent Splitting Methods Explained

Equal Split

The total rent is divided evenly across every roommate. This works best when bedrooms are similar in size and everyone earns a comparable income.

Split by Room Size

Each roommate's share is based on the square footage of their room relative to the total. A larger room means a larger share of the rent, and a smaller room means a smaller share.

Split by Income

Rent is divided proportionally based on what each roommate earns, so no one is paying a share of rent that strains their budget more than it strains anyone else's.

What About Utilities?

This calculator always splits utilities evenly across all roommates, separate from whichever rent method you choose, since shared costs like electricity and internet are usually consumed similarly by everyone in the home.

Which Method Should You Use?

  • Similar room sizes and similar incomes → Equal Split
  • Different room sizes, private bathrooms, or closets → Room Size Split
  • Roommates with very different incomes → Income Split

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this rent split calculator free?

Yes. It's completely free to use with no signup required.

How is the room-size split calculated?

Each roommate's rent share equals their room's square footage divided by the combined square footage of all rooms entered, multiplied by the total rent.

How is the income-based split calculated?

Each roommate's rent share equals their income divided by the combined income of all roommates entered, multiplied by the total rent.

Can I split utilities differently than rent?

This calculator splits utilities evenly by default, separate from the rent method you choose. For a full agreement covering utilities, house rules, and more, use the Roommate Agreement Generator below.
