Which Calculator Do I Need?

Not sure where to start? Here is a quick guide to our calculators for renters.

  • Deciding what you can spend? Try the Rent Affordability Calculator.
  • Moving in or out in the middle of a month? Use the Prorated Rent Calculator.
  • Sharing a place? Use the Rent Split Calculator for rent and the Utility Split Calculator for shared bills.
  • Planning your budget for a move? Use the Moving & Move-In Cost Calculator.
  • Wondering about a deposit? See the Security Deposit Interest Calculator and our Security Deposit Checker.

Our calculators give general estimates based on the numbers you enter. They are not financial or legal advice, and your lease and local law control what you actually owe.
