How to Split Utilities Between Roommates

Shared bills such as electricity, gas, water, internet and trash are a common source of roommate disputes. A clear method agreed on in advance keeps things fair and avoids awkward conversations.

Three Common Ways to Split Bills

  • Equal split: the simplest option. It works well when everyone lives there the whole time and uses the home similarly.
  • By days lived there: useful when someone moves in or out mid-month or is away for part of the billing period.
  • Custom percentages: useful when roommates agree that some people should pay more, for example for a larger room or heavier use.

Who Owes Whom?

Often one person pays a bill and the others pay them back. Choose who paid each bill in the calculator and it will show each person's balance, then suggest a simple set of payments to settle up with the fewest transfers.

Tips for Sharing Utilities

  • Agree on the method before the first bill arrives.
  • Keep copies of bills and payment confirmations.
  • Decide whose name is on each account, and what happens when someone moves out.
  • Write down your agreement. Our Roommate Agreement Generator can help.
  • Check your lease. It may say how utilities are handled or whether they are included in rent.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split utilities fairly with roommates?

Pick a method everyone agrees on before the first bill. An equal split is simplest. If someone lives there for only part of the period, splitting by days lived is fairer. Write down the agreement.

How do I split utilities if someone moves in mid-month?

Use the days method and enter how many days each person lived in the home during the billing period. Each person's share is based on their days.

How do I split internet between roommates?

Internet is commonly split equally because everyone can use it. If usage differs a lot, agree on custom percentages.

What if one roommate pays all the bills?

Select who paid each bill. The calculator subtracts what each person paid from their share and shows who owes whom.

Should utilities be split the same way as rent?

Not necessarily. Rent is often split by room size or income, while utilities are often split equally. You can use the Rent Split Calculator for rent and this tool for bills.

Is this tool legal advice?

No. It is a calculator for sharing costs. Your lease and any written roommate agreement control who is responsible to the landlord.
